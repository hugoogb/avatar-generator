# @avatar-generator/style-marks

## 3.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 619afe1: Four new styles. `robots` draws robots for bots, AI agents and service accounts. `portraits` draws single-colour ink portraits for professional tools. `creatures` draws friendly made-up monsters. `marks` draws logo-like badges for workspaces, teams and organisations, with an optional `letter` badge. Robots, Portraits and Creatures are layered, so their features mix with Faces, Illustrated and Anime through `mixStyles`. The CDN bundle includes all four.
